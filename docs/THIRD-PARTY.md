# Third-party tools

MediaOtter's own source is MIT licensed. Sidestream was inspected as an architecture reference. Its source, credentials, branding and service endpoints are not included in this repair.

- [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp#license): source and standalone build licensing differ. Consult the pinned release's notices, including its bundled dependencies.
- [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html): bundled static builds include GPL components. Retain the archive's license/build/source files and fulfill the applicable source availability requirements when publishing binaries.
- [Deno](https://github.com/denoland/deno/blob/main/LICENSE.md): MIT licensed, with additional components and notices in the upstream project.
- Adobe CSInterface and bundled fonts/icons retain their upstream notices.

Publisher releases must include notices and source availability required by the exact builds. File integrity checks do not replace this distribution step.

Questions

Is this legal / will my account get flagged?

MediaOtter downloads content for your own editing projects — the same way any downloader works. It uses the same public streams your browser plays; nothing is broken. Always respect content licenses and platform terms for anything you publish.

Why do I need my own Google credentials?

So nothing is shared. Instead of trusting a third party with one shared key, you create a free Google Cloud project (5 minutes) and the panel uses your Client ID and API key, stored only on your machine. This is what lets you sign into your own YouTube account for playlists.

Does it work in both Premiere Pro and After Effects?

Yes — one panel, both hosts. Premiere imports into a bin (and can add to the active sequence); After Effects imports footage and can add it to the active comp.

What about age-restricted or private videos?

Enable the browser session in Settings and MediaOtter borrows cookies from your own browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave or Firefox) — nothing is stored or uploaded.

Will you ever charge or add telemetry?

No, and the code proves it. MIT licensed — you can read every line. There is no server, no analytics, no account on our side.

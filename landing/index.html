Your YouTube, signed in

Sign in with your own Google account.

Browse your own playlists and liked videos without leaving Premiere. OAuth is strictly youtube.readonly — view-only access to your content.

  • Your playlists, liked videos, and subscriptions
  • Token stored locally at ~/.mediaotter/auth.json
  • Optional browser session for private / age-restricted videos
  • Nothing ever touches our servers — there are none
Scope is minimal: youtube.readonly only. MediaOtter can read your playlists and liked videos — it cannot upload, comment, or modify your account. Revoke anytime in Google Account permissions.

macOS · one line

Install from your terminal.

Paste this into Terminal. It downloads the right build for your Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel), installs it into the Adobe CEP extensions folder, and enables unsigned-extension loading — no admin password, no Java, no signing tools.

curl -fsSL https://github.com/madebysaira/mediaotter/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh

Then restart Premiere Pro / After Effects and open Window → Extensions → MediaOtter.

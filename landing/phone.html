MediaOtter is a Mac desktop extension that downloads media straight into Premiere Pro and After Effects. It can't be installed from a phone — so send the install page (and its one-line command) to your Mac instead.

What you'll get on your Mac
$curl -fsSL https://mediaotter.madebysaira.me/install.sh | sh

On your Mac you paste that one line, press Enter, restart Premiere — done. Free, open source, no admin password.

Send via WhatsApp

To WhatsApp Web or Desktop on your Mac — or message yourself and open it there. The message is pre-filled with everything you need.

Open it on your desktop

Copy the desktop link and paste it into Safari, Chrome or any browser on your Mac — the page also carries the one-line install command.

What is MediaOtter?

A media downloader for Premiere Pro & After Effects: search YouTube, paste any link, and import MP4/WAV straight to your timeline.

It's a Mac app

MediaOtter runs on macOS with a recent Photoshop, Premiere Pro, or After Effects (CEP 10). Windows is not supported.

The one-line command

Downloads the correct build (Apple Silicon or Intel), installs it into the Adobe CEP extensions folder, and enables unsigned-extension loading. No admin password, no Java, nothing else.

Free & private

MIT licensed. No accounts, no telemetry, no servers — everything runs on your Mac.

After install

Quit and reopen Premiere Pro or After Effects, then:

  1. Window → Extensions → MediaOtter
  2. Sign into YouTube once, securely, on your Mac
  3. Search, paste, import — done

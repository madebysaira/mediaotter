MediaOtter Mac desktop app
Premiere Pro & After Effects

You're on your phone. MediaOtter lives on your Mac.

MediaOtter is a macOS desktop extension — it downloads media straight inside Premiere Pro and After Effects. It can't be installed from a phone, so pick one way below to send the link to your desktop.

What you'll get: the install page plus a one-line Terminal command. On your Mac you paste the command, press Enter, restart Premiere — done. Free, open source, no admin password needed.

Send to your email

Works with any mail app on this phone

Type the email address you check on your Mac and tap send. Your mail app opens with a ready-to-send message containing the desktop link and the one-line install command — open it on your Mac and you're set.

Open it on your desktop

Copy the link, paste it on your Mac

Tap copy, then on your Mac open Chrome or Safari and paste the address. Or if you're on a phone browser right now: tap the menu (⋮ or ☰) and choose "Desktop site" to see the full page here.

https://mediaotter.madebysaira.me
Link copied — paste it in your Mac's browser.
  • 1Open Terminal on your Mac (Cmd+Space, type "Terminal")
  • 2Paste: curl -fsSL https://github.com/madebysaira/mediaotter/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh
  • 3Press Enter — it installs in about a minute
  • 4Restart Premiere Pro / After Effects → Window → Extensions → MediaOtter

What is what

MediaOtterThe media downloader for Premiere Pro & After Effects — search YouTube, paste any link, import MP4/WAV straight to your timeline.
It's a Mac appRequires macOS and a recent Photoshop, Premiere Pro or After Effects (CEP 10). Windows isn't supported yet.
The one-line commandDownloads the correct build (Apple Silicon or Intel) and installs it automatically — no admin password, no Java, nothing else to install.
Free & privateMIT licensed, no accounts, no telemetry, no servers. Everything runs on your Mac.
After installQuit and reopen Premiere Pro or After Effects, then open Window → Extensions → MediaOtter to start using it.
